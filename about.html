About Us

Learn more about our mission, our history, and the people behind Paws Shelter.

Our History

Paws Shelter is a community-based non-profit organisation established to rescue, shelter, and rehome stray, abandoned, and surrendered animals within the local area. Since our founding, we have relied on community donations and volunteer support to provide veterinary care, food, and safe housing to animals in need.

Mission and Vision

Mission: To rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome animals while promoting responsible pet ownership.

Vision: A community in which every companion animal has access to a safe, loving, and permanent home.

Our Team

Our shelter is run by a dedicated team of staff and volunteers who care for the animals every day, ensuring each one receives the attention, medical care, and love they deserve while waiting for their forever home.
